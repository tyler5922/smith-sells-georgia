About Tyler

Real estate since 2016. Your neighbor in Acworth.

Tyler began his real estate career in 2016 and spent five years as a licensed professional in Illinois, working across brokerage, leasing and management. His Illinois practice centered on commercial brokerage and property management, and he was a member of the Illinois Association of REALTORS®, ICSC and IREM.

Over those years Tyler represented families, investors, hospitals, development companies, and community groups pursuing municipal funding. He then stepped away from licensing for four years to work in the tech start-up world, while continuing to manage his own real estate holdings.

Today Tyler is licensed in Georgia with Watkins Real Estate Associates and serves buyers and sellers across the North Atlanta area. He earned his Bachelor of Business Administration from Lee University and his MBA from Benedictine University.

Tyler lives in Acworth with his wife and their three kids. When he isn't with clients you'll find him out on a run or watching football.
